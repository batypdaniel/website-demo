Our Story

Why I Started {{ client.name }}

With more than seven years of experience building data pipelines, machine learning models and software solutions, I have worked for companies of all sizes, from a small business struggling to keep up with the ever-increasing demands of its own growth, to large corporations that are the very best at building out their data and operations to a huge scale. After seeing both the problems and the solutions up close, I started {{ client.name }} to help bridge that gap.

We support business owners and teams with the data, AI and software foundations of their business, bringing big-business scaling and resources to help growing companies expand and thrive, without getting bogged down in administrative overhead. We aim to take work off your plate so that you can focus on what you do best: creating, building relationships, serving your customers, and leading your business.

We prioritize clearly defined projects that deliver meaningful, measurable value in a short time frame. It is our goal to make technology accessible and understandable, and we spell out exactly what you can expect before you commit to working with us.

Who I Am

Born and raised in {{ client.address.city }}, I have always been an analytical thinker, interested in building, creating, understanding how things work, and putting together systems with a lot of moving parts. As I have grown into my career, those ways of thinking have led me to developing different kinds of technology, mostly applying data science and AI to automating business operations and making them more efficient.

Now living in the Cooper-Young neighborhood, I have been inspired by the culture that has grown around and between the area's small businesses, and how they can even become pillars of their community. To whatever extent I can, I want to bring my skills and experience to contribute to that success, and to help local businesses thrive in a competitive environment.

I love to learn, teach, solve problems, collaborate with others, and help them succeed at whatever they do best. I started {{ client.name }} seeking a career where I can do all of those things, and I bring those motivations and values to every client and project. If you're unsure about our services or whether we can help, I am happy to have a conversation to share thoughts, ideas, and perspectives and to see if we might be a good fit for you.
